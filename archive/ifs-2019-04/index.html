Ingress First Saturday // April 2019

IngressFS – April 2019 Results

来源 Fev Games 原文 fevgames.net/ifs-04-2019/ 存档于 2026-11-03

12,384 Agents from 354 cities in 70 countries earned 1.467 Billion AP, gained 1,141 levels and walked 38,044 km during April’s First Saturday events around the world.

This month, the Enlightened had the most agents (+70), most levels gained (+63), most AP earned (+41.8m), most AP per agent (+5.4k), most KM walked (+460) and most KM per agent (+0.04)
